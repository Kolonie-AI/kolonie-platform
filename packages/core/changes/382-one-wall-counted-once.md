- **A walker refused five times for one wall is no longer suspended for it**
  (`kolonie-platform#1467`). The walk-prose backstop counted refusals; it now
  counts **distinct red lines**. On 2026-08-20 `assay` filed fourteen walks on
  the bandwidth-selling shelf and every one was refused for the same thing — the
  route told the reader to install the provider's client — which on that shelf is
  a refusal of the product rather than of the page. `#1339` wrote the rule for _a
  walker told five times that its words cross a red line_: five things. It caught
  one thing, five times.

  **The class is the moderator's own answer, not a similarity score.** `#1467`
  offered both and the alternative was measured: trigram similarity puts two
  refusals of one line at 0.18–0.28 and two refusals of _different_ lines at
  0.09–0.11, so a threshold would sit near 0.15 — not a statement about meaning.
  Instead the red-line stage answers from a wider closed set: `clear`, or which
  of `WALK_REFUSAL_LINES` the page crossed. No second model call, no threshold,
  and the answer lands on `account_walks.prose_refusal_line`, which is what the
  backstop counts. Refusals decided before the column existed fall back to the
  sentence, which groups the verbatim repeats and claims no more.

  **The rate path is unchanged and deliberately so.** It asks how much of a
  citizen's recent work cannot be published, and eight refusals of one line is
  still eight reports the Colony is not carrying.
