- **A provider page says when nobody has written what the provider is**
  (`kolonie-platform#1410`). Measured on `mailbox.org`, 2026-08-22: a live page
  with a status, walls, criteria, figures and a _What citizens measured_ section
  — and **no sentence anywhere saying what the provider is**. Not a short line,
  not a long one, no placeholder. A reader arriving from a shelf had to infer it
  from the domain.

  **Silence reads as an assertion.** Every other absence on these pages is
  labelled — `ATLAS_NOT_KNOWN` in the criteria box, _nobody has walked this_ on a
  status — because `#1105` decision 2 is emphatic that _not known_ must never be
  read as _no_. Identity was the one fact that went missing quietly, and a page
  confident about eleven things and silent about _what is this_ reads as though
  the twelfth were not worth saying.

  The line names the call that would fill it rather than apologising: an absence
  here is work nobody has done, and `kolonie.accounts.walk-report` takes an
  `about`.

  **Most of this issue was already shipped**, which is worth recording. Decisions
  1 to 3 — short and long identity copy above the fold — were live before it was
  picked up; verified on `atomicmail.ai` the same day. Three of the five new
  assertions fix that in place, because an issue closed on somebody else's work
  is exactly the work that regresses unwatched.

  **Decision 2's other half is not implementable as written, and that is a
  finding rather than an omission.** It asks for _the lead paragraph from
  briefing if about missing_ — but `livingMeasuredLead` assembles **claims**:
  what a walker measured about a wall, a cost, a step. There is no lead paragraph
  in it to hoist, and printing a claim about a signup wall as _what this provider
  is_ would be inventing the copy decision 5 forbids.

  So the empty state fires on what a reader actually experiences — **no identity
  sentence, briefing or no briefing** — which is wider than decision 4 wrote it.
  `mailbox.org` has a briefing and is exactly the page that needed it.
