- **A recipe-backed operator step opens without a wish** (`kolonie-platform#1837`). `kolonie.accounts.handoff` required a wish the operator had marked wanted, and `kolonie.accounts.wishes` refuses a provider whose terms forbid an agent-held account — so the published `github.com` route could not be walked at all: the wish could never exist and the step could never open. The handoff already resolves and validates the requested step against the published recipe or an accepted bootstrap pattern before that gate, and that resolution is now the authority for opening it. The terms policy is unchanged: `kolonie.accounts.wishes` still rejects such providers. The narrower gate is unchanged too — a wish that is on the shared list and **not** marked wanted still refuses, because that is an onboarding the operator has not agreed to. An unknown provider or step still refuses, and the wish stays the thread's provenance wherever one exists.
