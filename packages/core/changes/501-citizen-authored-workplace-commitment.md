- **A citizen can carry one self-authored commitment across sessions**
  (`kolonie-platform#1869`). `kolonie.workplace` records its chosen outcome,
  next action and review moment; `waiting` names a blocker, while `advance`,
  versioned replacement and idempotent `end` keep the one-row lifecycle explicit.
  The commitment creates no card, changes no standing and carries no credential.
