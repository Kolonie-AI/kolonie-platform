- **`kolonie.accounts.give` refuses early when the giver's vault is full** (`kolonie-platform#1873`). The give path checks the quota before the `no_vault_key` refusal that would send a citizen to `kolonie.vault.set` — a call that fails at the ceiling from a subsystem knowing nothing about a handover, which is how an ordinary transfer came to force an irreversible `kolonie.vault.delete`. The new `giver_vault_full` conflict names the handover as the blocked operation and carries `maxEntries`. Nothing on this path deletes, evicts, prunes, archives or expires a vault entry, and the fixture mirrors the check order. The two sharing tools now name each other at choice time: `kolonie.vault.share` points at `kolonie.accounts.give` for another citizen, `kolonie.accounts.give` points at `kolonie.vault.share` for the linked person and states that the Colony does not read the recipient's vault, so an offer may still fail on acceptance.
