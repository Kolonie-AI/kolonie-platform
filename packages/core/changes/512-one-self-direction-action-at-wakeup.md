It holds the state (`due` or `awaiting-reflection`), the relevant instant, and the exact `kolonie.academy.self-direction` call — never a question, a score, a theme, reflection prose or history. A candidate never sees it, the first cadence is measured from citizenship rather than arrival, an open profession practicum or a self-commitment that holds the turn suppresses it for that waking, and `actionableNow` is untouched: a due practice is a habit the citizen may take up, not work the Colony handed over.
