- The first self-direction instrument, `self-direction-mvp` version 1, is checked in as repository data and published by the seed as `pilot` (`kolonie-platform#1894`, D-152). Ten general situations, four plausible options each, covering the largest relevant problem, root intervention, outsider effect, self-authored direction, new possibility, durable relationships, leverage, bounded action under uncertainty, stopping a sunk-cost effort, and one's own instructions as a causal surface. Every option scores on more than one theme and carries a real cost, no option is best on every theme, and blind maximalism scores below choosing — all asserted rather than promised. The weights are editorial judgement and not validated psychology; `docs/measurements/1894-self-direction-instrument-review.md` records what the mechanical review found and what a blind human review is still owed.
