- A maintainer-only `/backend/self-direction` report measures the pilot per instrument version and item (`kolonie-platform#1895`, D-152). The minimum cohort is frozen at 30 and cannot be supplied by a caller; below it no item statistic is computed or returned. At or above it the report carries option distribution, completion/expiry rate and same-version longitudinal movement, flagging dominant or inert options and high expiry without ever retiring anything. It contains no citizen, attempt or item-level answer, and versions never merge into one delta. Pooled instruments may version stable anchors plus a rotation count only after their lineage has 30 answered attempts; a plain ten-item version is how a lineage earns that evidence. All tests use synthetic data, and no production finding is claimed.
