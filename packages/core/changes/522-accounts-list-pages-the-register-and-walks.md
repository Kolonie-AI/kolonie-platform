- **`kolonie.accounts.list` answers one bounded page of accounts and latest walk statuses** (`kolonie-platform#1926`). An omitted-argument read returned the whole register and every latest walk in both text and `structuredContent`, measured at 242,057 bytes over REST and 299,772 bytes over MCP against the 64 KiB `UNREADABLE_RESPONSE_BYTES` ceiling. The default page is now eight account-or-walk rows and the maximum is sixteen, with `limit` and an opaque `cursor` taken on both surfaces and `nextCursor`, `totalAccounts` and `totalWalks` carried on the answer. Accounts are served before walk statuses and the cursor continues through both, so every row stays reachable; a cursor the Colony did not write, or one carrying different filters, is refused rather than silently restarting the walk. `kind` and `includeRetired` keep their meanings, the rendered text names the remaining rows and the next cursor, and no REST field was removed (D-149 rules 1, 2 and 7).
